ENTERING THE FOREST
The story

Befriend the wild. Defend the farm.

You start at the edge of Wildwood with a camp tent, a rusty knife and a twig sling. Bunnies hop through the meadow, squirrels chatter in the Old Oak Grove and owls watch from the stumps at night. You don't catch them. You earn their trust.

Grow the food each animal loves, wait for its heart to fill, then carry your new friend home to its pen. Sell their gifts, forge better weapons from wolf fur and boar tusks, and build up your gates before the wolf packs raid the farm at night.

Wildwood is coming to Roblox.

Wildwood was made for Roblox from the start. The browser demo is a first taste of the world. The Roblox version is the real game, where you tame, survive and build together with your friends.
